❓ Help & About

👁️ Vision Mode

Turn on your camera. Tap "Describe what's around me" any time for a one-time description. Turn on "Live narration" for SenseBuddy to keep describing your surroundings automatically and warn you of hazards. Use "Read text or signs" to have any visible text read aloud. Ask a specific question anytime, by typing or using the mic.

🗣️ Voice Mode

Type or dictate a message, then tap "Speak it" and SenseBuddy will say it out loud for you. Use "Listen for a reply" to have someone's spoken response turned into text on screen.

🕘 History

Every description, answer, and message is saved here on your device so you can revisit it later. Nothing is sent anywhere except directly to Google's Gemini API for processing.

🔐 Privacy

SenseBuddy runs entirely through a private backend — you never need to enter any API key. Your camera image is sent securely for one-time processing and is never stored.
